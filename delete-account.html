Delete Account & Data

At Drive Test (developed by Mohsin Sapra), we value your privacy and provide you with full control over your personal data. If you no longer wish to use our services, you can request the deletion of your account and all associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request to have your account and associated data deleted by following these steps:

1. In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can initiate account deletion directly within the Drive Test app. This is the fastest way to process your request:

Step 1: Navigate to the Profile tab
Step 2: Tap on Edit Profile
Step 3: Select "Delete Account"

2. Via Email

Alternatively, you can send an email to support@drivetest.se with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Please include the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 30 days.

Types of Data Deleted

When you request account deletion, the following types of data will be permanently removed from our systems:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Study Progress: All your completed tests, bookmarks, and learning history.
  • Performance Analytics: Your personal charts, graphs, and activity logs.

Data Retention

Once an account deletion request is received and verified, we will process it within 30 days. Please note:

  • Certain data may be retained if required for legal, regulatory, or financial compliance (e.g., transaction records for premium subscriptions).
  • Non-identifiable, aggregated, and anonymized data may be kept for analytical purposes to improve our services.

If you have any questions regarding this process, please contact us at support@drivetest.se.
