Start Your Journey as a Professional Taxi Driver

Working as a professional taxi driver in Sweden is a great career opportunity. It usually takes two months to complete the process to get your taxi driver's license (taxiförarlegitimation) and the cost is 4,425 kr.

Requirements

The Swedish Transport Agency (Transportstyrelsen) issues licenses to individuals fulfilling the following requirements (at the time of application):

  • At least 21 years old
  • Have had a driver's license type B for minimum two years or have a driver's license type D
  • Fulfills the medical requirements necessary with regards to the safety of passengers and other road-users
  • Deemed to be suitable to work as a taxi driver with regards to professional qualifications and law obedience
  • Have passed a driving exam for taxi driver's license
  • Have passed 3 knowledge exams for taxi driver's license

How to Apply for Your Taxi Driver's License

1 Prepare Your Documents

Before starting the application process, make sure you have the following documents ready:

  • Valid driver's license (Type B for minimum 2 years, or Type D)
  • Proof of identity (Swedish ID or passport)
  • BankID for electronic verification

Having these documents ready will make the application process smoother and faster.

2 Pass the Exams at Trafikverket

You need to pass 4 different exams in total: 3 knowledge (theory) exams and 1 practical driving exam. When you feel ready to take all or one of the exams, you schedule an exam slot at Trafikverket's website through the link below. You can also contact Trafikverket's customer service at 0771-17 18 19.

Exam Types

Knowledge Exams (3 exams - 325 kr each):

  • Map Reading Exam - Tests your ability to navigate and read maps (considered the easiest, recommended to take first)
  • Traffic & Safety Regulations - Tests knowledge of Swedish traffic laws and taxi-specific regulations
  • Professional Knowledge - Tests customer service, professional conduct, and taxi industry knowledge

Practical Exam (1 exam):

  • Taxi Driving Exam - Practical driving test demonstrating professional taxi driving skills (1,200 kr if you need to borrow a vehicle with double steering)

Total Exam Fees:

  • 3 Knowledge exams: 975 kr (325 kr × 3)
  • 1 Driving exam: 1,200 kr (if you need to borrow a vehicle with double steering)
  • Total: 2,175 kr
  • Prices are valid weekdays, before 18:00

Please note: All exams are in Swedish.

Important Rules for the Exams:

  • You can pick the order of the exams yourself
  • To fulfill the professional qualifications requirement, all three knowledge exams need to be passed within a six month period from the first exam
  • The knowledge exams cannot be older than 3 years at the time of application
  • The driving exam cannot be older than 1 year at the time of application

Tips:

  • For the driving exam: Schedule your driving exam in time - the number of time slots are few and the waiting time is therefore often long.
  • For knowledge exams: Put together a time plan for your knowledge tests and take one exam at a time. Start with the Map reading exam, since that is considered the easiest.
  • Study strategy: Use the DriveTest app to practice with real exam-style questions for all three knowledge areas.

3 Complete a Medical Check

Visit your local medical centre to get a medical certificate (läkarintyg). This document certifies that you are medically fit to work as a professional taxi driver, and must be issued by a licensed doctor.

In Stockholm, we have a partnership with Medicinska Intyg, where you can book an appointment through the link below.

Cost: The cost of the certificate is typically between 800-1,000 kr

4 Apply for Your License

The final step is to submit your license application to Transportstyrelsen. If you are able to verify your identity electronically (e.g. through BankID), you can submit your application online using the link below.

You may be required to include complementary documentation in your application. For example, if you do not have a Swedish driver's license, you have to include a certificate from the country issuing the driver's license.

Application Costs:

  • Application fee: 1,200 kr
  • License production fee (if approved): 250 kr
