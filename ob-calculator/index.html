How OB is calculated — rates & rules ▾

Work = the gaps between your rest periods on the Vilotidsrapport. Each work slice is split at the 06:00 / 19:00 boundaries (and midnight), then priced by the time of day and the weekday:

BandWhen it appliesRate
OB2 Fri 19:00 → Sat 06:00 · Sat 19:00 → Sun 06:00 · Sun 19:00 → Mon 06:00 · public holidays & the day before a public holiday, 19:00 → 06:00 kr/h
OB1 Weekday nights (Mon–Fri) 19:00 → 06:00 · Saturdays & Sundays 06:00 → 19:00 · public holidays 06:00 → 19:00 kr/h
Normal Mon–Fri daytime 06:00 → 19:00 (not a holiday) — no OB premium —

A night belongs to its evening date: a 00:00–06:00 slice is owned by the previous evening, so a Friday-night shift stays OB2 even past midnight.

These two boundary times and the rates above apply to every photo; the weekday & holiday rules are unchanged. Override any single period's band in the result table.

Swedish public holidays ▾

These dates drive the holiday rule: a holiday daytime 06:00–19:00 counts as OB1, and the holiday night plus the evening before a holiday count as OB2. The list is computed automatically for any year (it follows Easter forward each year), so it's never tied to one year — just pick the year. Edit a date, remove one, or add your own; changes are saved per year in this browser and applied when you Calculate.
