ℹ️ How OB is calculated — rates & rules ▾

Work = the gaps between your rest periods on the Vilotidsrapport. Each work slice is split at 06:00 / 19:00 / midnight, then priced by the time of day and the weekday:

BandWhen it appliesRate
OB2 Fri 19:00 → Sat 06:00 · Sat 19:00 → Sun 06:00 · Sun 19:00 → Mon 06:00 · public holidays & the day before a public holiday, 19:00 → 06:00 52.79 kr/h
OB1 Weekday nights (Mon–Fri) 19:00 → 06:00 · Saturdays & Sundays 06:00 → 19:00 · public holidays 06:00 → 19:00 31.58 kr/h
Normal Mon–Fri daytime 06:00 → 19:00 (not a holiday) — no OB premium —

A night belongs to its evening date: a 00:00–06:00 slice is owned by the previous evening, so a Friday-night shift stays OB2 even past midnight. You can change the OB1 / OB2 rates in the data box, and override any single period's band in the result table.
