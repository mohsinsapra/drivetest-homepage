1. Who We Are

Drive Test is a mobile application designed to help individuals prepare for Swedish taxi and transport licensing exams (Taxiförarlegitimation and Yrkeskunnande). The app is developed and operated by:

  • Developer: Mohsin Sapra
  • App Name: Drive Test
  • Package ID: com.mohsinsapra.drivetest
  • Contact: support@drivetest.se

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve our services. This includes:

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address, display name, and authentication credentials.
  • Payment Information: When you subscribe to a premium plan, payment details are processed securely by our payment provider (Stripe). We do not store your full card number or banking details on our servers.
  • Support Communications: If you contact us via email, we retain that correspondence to assist you.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Study Progress & Test Results: Your completed tests, practice session scores, bookmarks, and learning history within the app.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the app, such as features used, session duration, and navigation patterns.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, app version, and unique device identifiers, collected to maintain app compatibility and performance.
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated and anonymized data on app usage collected via Google Analytics / Google Tag Manager to help us understand and improve the app experience.

2.3 Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your location data.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, or contacts.
  • We do not collect or store full payment card details.

3. Data Storage

We store your personal data on secure cloud infrastructure. Here is a clear breakdown of what we store and where:

3.1 What We Store

  • Account profile: Your name and email address, stored in Firebase Firestore under your unique user ID.
  • Authentication credentials: Your login credentials (email/password or third-party sign-in tokens) are managed and stored by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your raw password.
  • Study progress: Every test you take, your answers, scores, bookmarks, and session history are stored in our database so you can access them across devices and sessions.
  • Performance analytics: Your category scores, pass/fail history, activity logs, and progress charts are stored to power your personal dashboard.
  • Subscription status: Your active plan, subscription start/end dates, and renewal status are stored to manage your premium access.
  • Payment records: Transaction IDs and billing history (but not raw card details) are stored for financial record-keeping and support purposes.
  • Support emails: Any emails you send to support@drivetest.se are retained to help us resolve your issue.

3.2 Where We Store It

  • Firebase (Google Cloud): Your account, study progress, and analytics data are stored in Firebase Firestore and Firebase Authentication, hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure in the EU/US.
  • Stripe: Payment records and subscription data are stored on Stripe's PCI-DSS certified servers. We only retain a Stripe customer ID on our side — no raw card data.

3.3 How Long We Store It

Your data is stored for as long as your account is active. See Section 7 — Data Retention for full details on retention periods and what happens when you delete your account.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your user account.
  • Provide personalized study analytics, performance charts, and progress tracking.
  • Process subscription payments and manage premium access.
  • Send transactional emails related to your account (e.g., password reset, subscription confirmation).
  • Respond to your support inquiries and feedback.
  • Monitor and improve app performance, stability, and user experience.
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent activity or abuse of our services.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data for targeted advertising.

5. Third-Party Services

Drive Test integrates with the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data:

4.1 Firebase (Google)

We use Firebase for user authentication and cloud data storage. Firebase may collect device identifiers and usage data. See Firebase Privacy Policy.

4.2 Stripe

Stripe processes all payment transactions for premium subscriptions. Stripe handles your payment data in accordance with PCI-DSS standards. We receive only a tokenized reference, not your raw card details. See Stripe Privacy Policy.

4.3 Google Analytics & Google Tag Manager

We use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to collect anonymized usage statistics. This helps us understand how users interact with the app so we can improve the experience. See Google Privacy Policy.

4.4 Google Play & Apple App Store

Distributing the app through Google Play and (in the future) the Apple App Store means those platforms may collect data in accordance with their own privacy policies when you download or update the app.

6. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party processors (Firebase, Stripe, Google) strictly to provide our services as described above.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law, regulation, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business Transfer: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred. We will notify you before this occurs.
  • Safety: To protect the rights, property, or safety of Drive Test, our users, or the public.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. Specifically:

  • Active Accounts: Your account data and study progress are retained while your account remains active.
  • After Deletion: Upon account deletion, identifiable personal data is removed within 30 days.
  • Legal & Financial Records: Transaction records and billing information may be retained for up to 7 years as required by Swedish financial regulations.
  • Anonymized Data: Non-identifiable, aggregated data may be retained indefinitely for analytical and service improvement purposes.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your account and personal data. See our Delete Account page for instructions.
  • Restriction: Request that we limit the processing of your personal data.
  • Portability: Request a machine-readable export of your data.
  • Objection: Object to certain types of processing, including analytics.
  • Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@drivetest.se. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Security

We take the security of your personal data seriously. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Secure authentication via Firebase.
  • PCI-DSS compliant payment processing via Stripe.
  • Screenshot protection within the app to prevent unauthorized capture of exam content.
  • Access controls limiting who within our team can access user data.

While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

Drive Test is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@drivetest.se and we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
  • Notify registered users via email or an in-app notification where appropriate.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of Drive Test after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
