Subscriptions are managed through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). On iPhone: go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, select Drive Test, and tap Cancel Subscription. On Android: open Google Play → tap your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
Go to Settings → Restore Purchases inside the app. This re-validates your active subscription with the App Store or Google Play. If the issue continues after restoring, contact us and include your order number or a screenshot of your receipt.
Refunds are handled by Apple or Google depending on where you purchased. For App Store purchases, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google Play, open the Play Store → order history → tap the order → Request a refund. We're happy to assist if you have trouble.
